I'm a Computer Science student from Coimbatore, building LLM, RAG and NLP systems, with ML and DevOps as the next things on my list.

I like AI that can explain itself: answers with sources, models tested on data they have never seen, and results reported honestly even when they sting. Off the keyboard, I write cricket takes and am slowly rebuilding my story-book habit.
